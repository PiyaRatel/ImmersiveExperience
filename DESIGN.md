## What the document covers

1. **Product overview.** What Immersive Gamebox is: private rooms for up to 6 players, with projection, motion tracking and touch walls; 30- or 60-minute sessions; licensed games; pricing; 38 venues.
2. **Where the reviews come from.** Google, TripAdvisor, Yelp, press. Reddit and Trustpilot weren't covered.
3. **Top 5 review themes.**
   1. Fun for groups and families (positive)
   2. Friendly staff (positive)
   3. Expensive for an hour (negative)
   4. Tech glitches with visors and tracking (negative)
   5. Less immersive or deep than expected for adults (mixed)
4. **Research papers.** The five most-cited papers on play and engagement in sensor-based rooms with physical interaction: Exertion Interfaces, RoomAlive, Understanding the Role of Body Movement, IllumiRoom, and Movement-Based Game Guidelines.
5. **Games in the papers.** The games each paper tested and how players responded. Examples: Breakout for Two, RoomAlive Whack-A-Mole, Guitar Hero, JS Joust.

## Using it when planning

- Start design decisions from the review complaints (price/value, tech reliability, depth for adults) and the strengths (group play, staff).
- Back up design choices for physical interaction with the research findings. Two examples:
  - More body movement leads to higher engagement.
  - Physical exertion strengthens social bonding.
- If the document and this file disagree, the document wins. Update this file to match.
